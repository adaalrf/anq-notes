The next person might be a teammate, a visitor, or you after a long break.
Tell them where to start and what a successful result looks like.

## A simple checklist

- Explain the concrete purpose.
- Show one working example.
- Name the constraints that affect real use.
- Keep commands repeatable.

## Be precise about evidence

A local browser check is useful. It is not evidence of months of production
operation. Describing the boundary makes the work easier to trust.
